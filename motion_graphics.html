The Challenge

Post-Production Workflow

1. **Concept & Script**: Drafting the timeline hook to retain audiences.

2. **Visual Assets**: Sourcing and custom designing layouts in Illustrator.

3. **After Effects Animation**: Designing custom transitions, camera movements, and keyframe graphs.

4. **Audio Design**: Adding SFX and sound loops in Premiere Pro for immersive storytelling.
